# What’s new at Shah’s Nutrition

Small changes to make the order book easier to use, and to keep the kitchen and orders in step.

## October 2026

### Faster starts and a bill when you deliver
The Orders app checks for updates as soon as it opens, so the latest version is ready sooner. Mark an order Delivered and its bill is ready to share on WhatsApp or download; open it again from the order whenever you need it.

### See how it’s going
Home brings sales, money in, orders and packs together for today, this week, month, year or since we started, with a comparison to the time before. Profit is shown as an estimate.

## September 2026

### Hold an order in Cooking
Move a Packing or Ready order back to Cooking when plans change. Its cooked food stays reserved, and it waits for you to move it on to Packing.

### Orders follow the kitchen
Orders move through Cooking, Packing, Ready and Delivered. Once every item is cooked, an order moves to Packing. Log cooking records what was actually made, while spare stock stays visible until someone decides it is used up or thrown out.

### Priority, samples and payments
Priority orders get first call on cooking, with a check before a packed pouch is moved from another order. Free samples have their own pack size and list. Part payments can be recorded as they come in, with the remaining amount shown on the order.

### Prices and coupons for the order book
Prices can be set by product and pack size in the private admin. Coupons can have their own prices, and can be marked Repeat or One-time. Add order works out the total when prices are available; delivery is kept separate.

### The first release
Shah’s Nutrition launched with Raggi Jaggi, Muesli and Date Bites. Customers build an order on the site and send it to Sunit on WhatsApp; we reply in the chat with the total, delivery and how to pay. There’s no checkout or payment on the site.
